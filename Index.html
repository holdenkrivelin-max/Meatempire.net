FOR INVESTORS

The build layer.

MEAT Empire is the creation layer of the MEAT Universe — the infrastructure that lets chefs, operators, foodies, and builders test and launch ideas with real support behind them, instead of alone and unfunded.

It's early. There's no roster of funded creators yet, no revenue to report. What exists is the model: give people a lower-risk way to test real ideas with real people, and let the ones that work grow into something bigger — with MEAT Hospitality Group already proving the core loop works in its own dinners.

Every part of MEAT is built to strengthen the others. Empire doesn't need to be the highest-margin piece of the business to be a critical one — it's how the ecosystem keeps generating new things worth being part of.

The Sequence

Dinners prove the format repeats. Real partnerships make MEAT Points worth something real. The app gets built, simply at first. Empire opens up from there — growing on proven ground, not a bigger pitch.

The Differentiator

Most rewards ecosystems show everyone the same offers and hope something sticks. MEAT learns the person first, then personalizes what they see, who they meet, and which rewards actually mean something to them. Relevance before inventory — the right person, the right opportunity, one user at a time.

MEAT Grows As You Grow

Most apps recommend the same thing forever. MEAT keeps asking where you are, and where you'd like to be — then updates as the answers change. MEAT doesn't define creators by who they were when they signed up. It evolves with who they're becoming.

There for the milestones, and everything in between.

If Building Gets Off Track

Yesterday's step doesn't just disappear. Keep it today, move it, break it smaller, mark it complete, or drop it. If you keep moving the same thing, MEAT can ask: "You've moved this three times. Want me to make it easier?" Accountability without shaming.

A Little Mudge

A Mudge is a gentle, opt-in nudge about today's or tomorrow's MEATs — sent only if you've said you want it, even when your phone's off or on silent. A little Mudge. A real move.

Not daily minutes spent in MEAT. Daily progress made by MEAT.

Instagram says look at everybody else. TikTok says keep watching. MEAT says: "Hey, what are you building today?" And then actually helps.

MEAT helps you remember your work, organize your work, and actually build it.

Inside the App

What is Meaties Place?

One destination where every creator shows up — matched by shared interest, shared goals, and what you're actually building that day. It's the first tab in the app, right after Today.

From there: team up on something shared, or just show support — like, comment, and celebrate what someone else just shipped. Nobody connects automatically. The other person always decides — MEATUp, or MEATUp later.

Meates are interest-based communities inside MEAT. Each Meate brings together creators with overlapping ideas, industries, or goals. Connect with people already inside one, discover new creators, join relevant MEATUps, and share progress with a group — not just one creator at a time. Meates make a large network feel small, relevant, and personal.

Meaties Place rewards progress, not popularity. No follower counts, no status competition — someone's first idea matters just as much as someone's tenth launch.

Inside the App

Mudge: a personalized MEAT prompt that helps you remember, act, connect, or take your next First Step.
