FOR INVESTORS

The build layer.

MEAT Empire is the creation layer of the MEATVerse — the infrastructure that lets chefs, operators, foodies, and builders test and launch ideas with real support behind them, instead of alone and unfunded.

It's early. There's no roster of funded creators yet, no revenue to report. What exists is the model: give people a lower-risk way to test real ideas with real people, and let the ones that work grow into something bigger — with MEAT Hospitality Group already proving the core loop works in its own dinners.

Every part of MEAT is built to strengthen the others. Empire doesn't need to be the highest-margin piece of the business to be a critical one — it's how the ecosystem keeps generating new things worth being part of.

The Sequence

Dinners prove the format repeats. Real partnerships make MEAT Points worth something real. The app gets built, simply at first. Empire opens up from there — growing on proven ground, not a bigger pitch.

The Differentiator

Most rewards ecosystems show everyone the same offers and hope something sticks. MEAT learns the person first, then personalizes what they see, who they meet, and which rewards actually mean something to them. Relevance before inventory — the right person, the right opportunity, one user at a time.

MEAT Grows As You Grow

Most apps recommend the same thing forever. MEAT keeps asking where you are, and where you'd like to be — then updates as the answers change. MEAT doesn't define creators by who they were when they signed up. It evolves with who they're becoming.

Inside the App

What is MEAT Social?

MEAT Social is the entire people-and-connection layer of MEAT — every way you find, keep, and grow the relationships that matter to you, all in one place.

Shared plans, events, tasks, recommendations, and MEAT Moments all live inside that same social experience — so however you connect, it stays together instead of scattered.

MEAT Friends — people you already know · MEAT Intros — people MEAT thinks you should meet · MEAT Circles — groups built around shared interests · MEAT Match — romantic connection, fully opt-in

MEAT Social isn't about popularity. It's about participation, progress, and finding the right people to move forward with. 💚

We're all meating in the middle.

Inside the App

What are MEAT Circles?

Communities built around what matters to you.

Join Circles based on your interests, goals, activities, location, or what you're working toward. Connect with fellow Meaties, share progress, recommend things to each other, plan MEATUps, and help one another keep moving.

Whether it's fitness, food, business, creativity, travel, or something completely different, MEAT Circles gives you a smaller space inside MEAT Social to find people who are moving in a similar direction.

Not about popularity. About participation, support, and progress.

Inside the App

What are MEATings?

Your personalized reminders for the day.

MEATings gives you a gentle ping when something in your day needs your attention — a task, bill, goal, appointment, follow-up, First Step, or something you said you wanted to remember.

"Here are your MEATings for today." That's the whole idea — phone off, on silent, out living your life, MEATings only shows up if you've said you want it, and it only ever asks about what's actually yours.

MEATing: Your electric bill is due tomorrow.
MEATing: You wanted to call Mom after work.
MEATing: One First Step left toward today's goal.
MEATing: Long day? Check in before you plan tomorrow.

Not more notifications. Better ones.

Inside the App

What is MEAT Next?

Personalized guidance for what comes next — based on where you are, what you've done, and where you want to go.

MEAT Next is MEAT's personalized "what's next?" layer. After you finish what you already needed to do, MEAT Next helps you decide where to go from there based on your goals, interests, progress, routines, and what matters to you right now.

It can suggest the next step toward a goal, a follow-up from something you completed, an activity that fits your interests, a person or Circle to connect with, a MEATUp or experience, something you've been putting off, or a new direction when you're not sure what to do next. The fourth home in the app, right alongside Today, MEAT Social, and MEAT Rewards.

You handled today. Now let's figure out what's next.

Inside the App

What is MEAT Count?

A visible record of forward movement.

More than a step counter — every meaningful thing you did today, across everything you're building. Steps toward a launch, a bill paid, money moved toward a runway goal. Then MEAT shows both sides: what you did today, and where it's taking you — MEAT Count Today: 7, Runway Goal: 25% complete. A small win counts the same whether it's yours or someone else's bigger one. MEAT never ranks you against anyone else, only against your own progress.

Every step counts. Every payment counts. Every action counts. Because progress should lead somewhere. That somewhere is MEAT.

Making Ends MEAT
